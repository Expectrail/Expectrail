# 2026-11-14 Receipt v1, clearance is an allow-list (#255)

## What changed

`expectrail.receipt/v1`: schema, example, writer and reader, offline Ed25519 trust store, and `python -m expectrail.receipt trust-check`.

Clearance is an allow-list. It is granted only when every condition was positively shown:

- the issuer is in the trust store and the signature verifies with that key (a key found only inside the receipt is never trusted);
- `--repo`, `--expect-head` and `--expect-policy` were given and match (if one is omitted the report says "repository not checked", "head not checked" or "policy not checked", and clearance is refused);
- `issuedAt`..`expiresAt` contains now and the span is positive and at most 7 days;
- `coverage.required` is non-empty (`minItems: 1` in the schema, and `check_receipt` refuses an empty list);
- every required check's result is `"pass"` (`fail`, `error` and `not-exercised` all refuse) and `exercised` covers `required`;
- evidence grade is `full` and the decision is `accept`.

Anything not shown is listed in `does_not_establish`. A valid signature on insufficient evidence still reports "no clearance".

This replaces #426. The earlier checker treated a missing expectation as a pass, and treated any result key as completeness.

Two existing expectations changed, because they granted clearance without the caller naming the repository, head and policy. `Guard: test change intended (#255)`.

`expectrail/contracts.py` is unchanged, so the discovery freeze holds. No workflow change.
