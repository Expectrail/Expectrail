# The no-model sensor can judge pixel checks about the bullet (#286)

## What changed
`bullet-behind-scenery` (the bullet's `z_index` from 5 to -5) could only be judged through SAM, which is
paid for each image. The sensor's templates never matched the bullet. Looking at real frames showed four
reasons, none of them its size:

- It is a RigidBody2D and turns as it flies and bounces, so its art is drawn rotated.
- An additive glow is drawn over it, so no rendered pixel is in its art's palette.
- `physics_interpolation` is on in the game, and the bullet moves about 14 frame pixels a tick. Each frame
  draws it 1 to 1.7 ticks behind the box logged on the frame's tick, under the frame's own camera.
- Its 1 s timer calls `destroy`, which fades it out over 0.3 s.

What was built:

- `agents/template_radial.py` (new): a witness for a round sprite that turns. It reads the mean colour of
  each one-pixel ring around the sprite's centre, which does not change when the sprite turns.
  - `paths` gives each logged bullet's path in a frame: its box two ticks back (or the first one logged after
    that) to its box on the frame's tick, both through the frame's camera, and its age in ticks.
  - `calibrate` freezes ring profiles from the clean calibration replays only. The approved art locates the
    sprite on its path by luminance correlation (at least 0.85). It then validates on the separate clean
    replay with the player's rule (95% of positives, at most 1% of displaced negatives). A crop that touches
    another logged box, a fading bullet and one partly on screen are left out.
  - `look` searches only along the path. It gives `matched` when some position has rings within 12 (mean
    absolute RGB, fixed before validation) of a profile, and `not-found` when none does. A fading bullet, one
    whose path leaves the screen, or one not the calibrated size is a gap.
  - `check_witness` reads it back: digest, ring count, the fixed settings, and a status its counts give.
- Contract `template-bank`: an optional `radialWitnesses.bullet`, and an optional `witness: "radial"` on
  `calibration.objects.bullet` (also in `observations`, which copies the calibration). New example
  `contracts/examples/template-bank-foreground-radial.json`, from a synthetic calibration. The writer is
  `template_observer.add_radial`, called by `calibrate_bank` (jit.yml's `sensor-bank` job). The reader is
  `template_foreground.check_bank`, which refuses a witness with no record, a record with no witness, and a
  role that has both a witness and templates.
- `expectrail/jit/template_observer.py`: `RADIAL_OBJECTS` (the bullet: its art, 32-pixel side, fades after
  120 ticks). The observer is pointed at the bullet when its witness is `met`, and `_look_radial` gives rows
  of the same shape as the enemy's.
- `expectrail/jit/pixel.py`: `OBSERVERS["visual-sensor"]["boxes"]` is now `("enemy", "bullet")`, so the
  proposer is offered bullet checks for the sensor.
- `tools/bullet_visible_stand_in.py`: `--observer visual-sensor` writes a stand-in for the sensor, with
  windows cut short of fading bullets (`SENSOR_PROBES`). The SAM stand-in it writes is byte for byte as before.
- `bench/jit/stand-in/bullet-behind-scenery-sensor/`: that stand-in, its real-engine record and its page.
- Docs: `docs/jit.md`, a new section "The bullet, for the no-model sensor". `docs/measurements/271-pixel-counting.md`
  regenerated by `counting.pixel_bench`: 3 records, 8 pixel checks, 0 counting. The two sensor bullet checks
  meet rules 1, 3 and 4.

## What was run
- On the real engine (Godot 4.7.2 here, Xvfb, no model, not in CI):
  - Hand probes of clean and bug frames on `move-fire-right`, `move-fire-left` and `shoot-left-every12`. They
    showed the rotation, the glow and the lag. The prototype ring witness was within 3 of the clean bullet at
    the 95th percentile on the two probes it was not built from, and nothing else on screen came within 33.
  - `python3 -m expectrail.jit.cli sensor-bank` (8 min 6 s):
    - 556 templates (408 player and 148 enemy, as before);
    - the bullet: one ring profile, 70 of 70 validation positives and 0 of 70 negatives (`met`);
    - bank digest `58f45c41...`.
    - The first calibration, before the path used the frame's camera, was `unmet` (66 of 70). The 4 misses
      were one bullet lying still while the camera scrolled 14 pixels a tick.
  - `jit run --proposal` with the sensor stand-in, `--observer visual-sensor --withhold-candidate`, against
    `no-fix.patch` (7 min 48 s, 18 replays). Both checks were `kept`: clean pass 3 of 3, bug fail 3 of 3, bug
    plus no-fix fail 3 of 3. $0 was reserved for the observer.
    - An earlier run with the SAM stand-in's full windows dropped all three checks as `needs-review`, because
      fading bullets were on screen.
- Unit tests: `python3 -m unittest tests.test_jit_sensor_bullet tests.test_bullet_visible_stand_in
  tests.test_jit_pixel tests.test_jit_sensor_named tests.test_jit_template_observer tests.test_jit_sensor_facing`
  passed. There are 21 new tests in `tests/test_jit_sensor_bullet.py` and 2 new tests in the stand-in's tests.
- Full suite: see the pull request.

Nothing was dispatched and nothing was spent.
