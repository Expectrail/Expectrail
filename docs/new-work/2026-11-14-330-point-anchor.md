# Ask the vision model with a point, not a box (#330)

## What changed

- The first live calibration on kinematic-character (run 43609849950, $0.095, 38 images) was not
  used: found 100%, size 42%, stable 0%. Its rows showed the model found the 16 px sprite (answers 16
  to 19 px) with the normal box. But the box twice as large took in the tiles around it (30 to 62 px),
  and 25% of the 14 px collision box is 3.5 px.
- Sean's decision (#330, November 14): ask with a point at the middle of the collision box; size
  within 25% or 4 px; stable: a point nudged 3 px, still inside the collision box, gives the same
  box (within 25% or 3 px). The 90% thresholds are unchanged.
- "The same box" also means it moved less than the point did. Without that, a model that draws a
  player-sized box around whatever point it is asked about would pass (the `Echo` stand-in).
- `expectrail/anchored.py`: point questions in `calibrate` (both points in one request: 19 images for
  a calibration, not 38) and in `AnchoredObserver.look`; the nudge goes inward at the image's edge.
- `contracts/vision-calibration.v1.schema.json`: the new rules (`sizeFloorPx`, `stableFloorPx`,
  `nudgePx`; no `wideFactor`), `observer.prompt: point` (no `pad`), and `nudgedSize` rows. Example
  regenerated.
- `contracts/vision-recording.v1.schema.json` and `expectrail/recorded.py`: a `segment-at` answer
  kind, so point answers are recorded and replayed (writer `RecordingClient.segment_at`, reader
  `RecordedClient.segment_at`).
- `tools/outside_vision.py`: the summary names the nudge test. `docs/outside-projects.md`: the new
  rules and the first live calibration.

## What was run

- `tests.test_anchored_observer` and `tests.test_outside_vision`: 26 tests, OK, with stand-in models
  only: one that sees the player (used), one that echoes the point (refused by the nudge), one that
  answers with the tiles (refused by size), one that answers nothing, and the recording round trip.
